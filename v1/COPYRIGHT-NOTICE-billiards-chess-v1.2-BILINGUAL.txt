三、v1.2 版本说明
1. 联网模式采用 1～10 号固定房间，每个房间最多 10 人，其中最多 2 名执棋者，其余为旁观者。

3. 换位权限默认关闭；是否允许换位由房间内白方或黑方执棋者设置，旁观者不能修改该权限。

3. v1.2 Release Notes
1. Online mode uses fixed rooms 1–10. Each room supports up to 10 connected clients, with at most 2 players and the remaining clients as spectators.
2. Room names, nicknames, room status, and the related online-room logic are part of this release's implementation.
3. Swap permission is disabled by default. It can be enabled or disabled only by the White or Black player in the room; spectators cannot change this permission.
4. Client-side JavaScript must still be delivered to and executed by the browser, so client code cannot be made absolutely confidential.

Room-unified settings / 房间统一设置
--------------------------------
The v1.2 online room system may synchronize rule-affecting settings from the room server to connected players, including piece size, knight pass-through and avoidance radius, custom weights, capture-angle tolerances, and clock rules. Animation speed and display glyph preferences remain client-local.

v1.2 联网房间规则设置可由房间服务器统一同步到已连接玩家，包括棋子大小、马可穿透及避碰范围、自定义重量、吃子角度容差和计时规则；动画速度与棋子图标等显示设置保持各端独立。
